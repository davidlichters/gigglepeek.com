Frequently asked questions

When will my calendar arrive?

Orders are processed within 14 business days. Standard delivery then takes 14 to 21 business days. You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Is it gift-ready?

Comes in its printed calendar box, ready to wrap.

How much is shipping?

Free shipping on every order. No extra cost is added at checkout.

What is inside?

24 squishy cubes in solid, jelly, glitter and snowflake styles in bright colors.

How big are the box and the cubes?

The box is 14.17 x 9.45 in (36 x 24 cm). Each cube is about 1.38 in (3.5 cm).

What age is it for?

Ages 6 and up. Small parts: not for children under 6.

What are the cubes made of?

Soft, squeezable thermoplastic elastomer (TPE). They spring back to their shape after every squeeze.

What if we start late?

No problem: catch up on the cubes you missed, then go on with one a day.

What if my child does not like it?

30-day money-back guarantee: not happy with your calendar? Email us within 30 days of delivery and we refund you in full.

Where do you ship?

United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand.

Can I order 4 or more?

Yes. Choose "4+ calendars" above the cart button and set the number you need: each one costs $25.

How do I reach you?

Email hello@gigglepeek.com. We answer every email, Monday to Friday.
